LizzBot Health

LizzBot Health is a private, personal tool. It reads activity, sleep and vital-sign data from the Google Health API so the account holder’s own assistants can show them their own Fitbit and Google Health summaries.

It is not a public product. Only the account holder authorises access, and only their own Google Account is used. The tool requests read-only Google Health scopes for activity and fitness, sleep, and health metrics and measurements. It does not write, share, or sell that data.

Data is stored only on the account holder’s private server so their assistants can build a daily summary for them. Access can be revoked at any time in Google Account permissions.

Read the Privacy Policy for how this app accesses, uses, stores and shares Google user data.
