Privacy Policy

Effective 25 November 2026. This policy is for LizzBot Health, a personal app used only by the Google Account holder who authorises it.

Who we are

LizzBot Health is a private integration that reads the account holder’s Google Health and Fitbit data so their own assistants can prepare personal daily summaries. It is not offered to the public. Questions: lizzbot@mail.grokbot.com.

What Google user data we access

With the account holder’s Google Account consent, the app requests only these read-only Google Health API scopes:

  • Activity and fitness data (for example steps, active minutes, calories and exercise sessions)
  • Sleep data (duration and sleep stages)
  • Health metrics and measurements (for example resting heart rate, heart-rate variability, blood oxygen and breathing rate)

The app does not request write access and does not modify Google Health data.

How we use that data

We use the data solely to generate a private daily health summary for the account holder and to help their personal assistants answer questions about their own activity, sleep and vitals. We do not use Google user data for advertising, analytics products, training public models, or any purpose other than this personal summary.

How we store that data

Access tokens and refresh tokens are stored in restricted files on the account holder’s private server. Daily summaries are stored on the same server with restricted file permissions. Data is not stored in public repositories.

How we share that data

We do not sell Google user data. We do not share it with third parties, advertisers, or other people. It is visible only to the account holder and to the private assistants they operate on their own server. We will not transfer Google user data to others except if required by law.

Limited use

Use of data received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Retention and deletion

Summaries are kept only as long as they are useful for the account holder’s personal record. They can revoke the app’s access at any time at Google Account permissions. After revocation, stored tokens are deleted and the app can no longer read their Google Health data.
